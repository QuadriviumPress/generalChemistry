# General Chemistry: Principles, Patterns, and Applications

This textbook develops the principles of chemistry through patterns in chemical behavior and applications to everyday life. The complete edition contains 24 chapters, worked examples, conceptual and numerical problems, selected answers, and nine reference appendices.

Begin with [Introduction to Chemistry](chapters/ch-01-introduction-to-chemistry.md), or read the [Preface](front/preface.md). Use the book navigation to explore atomic structure and bonding, chemical reactions, energy, equilibrium, and the chemistry of the elements.

[Energy Changes in Chemical Reactions](chapters/ch-05-energy-changes-in-chemical-rea.md) covers energy and work, enthalpy, calorimetry, nutrition, and energy sources.
